Android early access

Join early. Play free.

Puzzle Lantern is coming to Google Play. Early players get every game free, with no subscription. Joining takes three quick steps.

Use the same Google account in all three steps: the group, the early access page and Google Play on your Android phone.

  1. STEP 1

    Join the early access group

    Open the group and choose Join group. Already a member? Go to step 2.

    Join the group ↗

  2. STEP 2

    Turn on early access in Google Play

    Open the early access page and tap the Become a tester button. Google Play lets you install the app after this step.

    Turn on early access ↗

  3. STEP 3

    Install and play

    Open the store listing on your Android phone, tap Install and open the app. Keep it installed to keep your free access.

    Install from Google Play ↗

Can’t see the app?

Check which Google account is selected in Groups and Google Play. After you join, allow a few minutes for access to update, then open the early access page again. The app does not appear in Play search yet.

Send us feedback

Early players shape what comes next. Send us an idea or a problem from the app’s Settings, or email tashukwork@gmail.com.

You can leave early access from the same Google Play page at any time. Read our privacy policy.
