Android early access

A little early. A lot to play.

Puzzle Lantern is in an early Google Play test. There are a couple of quick steps before you can install it. Thanks for helping us make it better.

We’re preparing the first Android release. Enrollment links will appear here as soon as the test is ready. Check back soon.

Use the same Google account for the tester group, the test opt-in and Google Play on your Android device.

  1. STEP 1

    Join a tester group

    Open one of the groups below and choose Join group. You only need to join one. Already a member? Continue to step 2.

    Puzzle Lantern Testers ↗

  2. STEP 2

    Become a tester on Google Play

    After joining a group, open the test page and choose Become a tester. Joining the group alone does not enroll you in the Play test.

  3. STEP 3

    Install and play

    Open the store listing on your Android device and tap Install. Try a Daily, explore the games and let us know what feels right or needs work.

Can’t see the app?

Check which Google account is selected in Groups and Google Play. After joining, allow a little time for access to update, then reopen the test link. The app may not appear in Play search during testing.

Help us test

Please stay opted in for at least 14 days and play regularly if you can. Try notifications after completing a Daily, add a friend, and tell us about confusing screens or crashes. Notifications are optional; you can change them in Settings → Notifications.

Send feedback from the app’s Settings, or email tashukwork@gmail.com. Include your device model and Android version if reporting a problem.

You can leave the test from the same Google Play test page at any time. Read our privacy policy.
