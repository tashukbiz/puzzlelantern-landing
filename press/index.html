Puzzle Lantern press & product information

Puzzle Lantern brings daily Sudoku, word and memory games together in one iPhone and iPad app. Each game has a shared Daily challenge, so friends can play the same puzzle and compare results.

Four games, four different puzzles

  • Sudoku: fill a 9×9 grid so each row, column and box contains the digits 1 to 9.
  • Recall: watch a grid pattern, then tap the cells you remember.
  • Vanish: study a hand of cards and identify the card that disappears.
  • Word Sense: find three hidden words from sentence prompts, revealing clues when needed.

How the daily collection works

Each game has one Daily challenge shared by everyone playing that puzzle date. Players can keep track of their results and streaks, and compare results on friends and global leaderboards. Sudoku, Recall and Vanish also offer More puzzles for additional rounds. Word Sense currently offers the Daily challenge.

There is no signup form or password. The app creates an account with a generated username. Friends connect through an invite link or a request to an exact username, with acceptance from the other person. The app does not ask for access to contacts.

Puzzle Lantern is made for entertainment. Game names describe what players do; scores are not measurements of cognitive ability.
