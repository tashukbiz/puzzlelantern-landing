Vanish: Find the Missing Card Game

Vanish is a missing-card memory game in Puzzle Lantern. Study a hand of playing cards, then look at the hand again with one card removed. Choose the missing card from five options. You are remembering card identities, not playing poker or making bets.

Free to download for iPhone and iPad. See the app for current access and subscription options. Android is coming soon.

How to play Vanish

  1. Study each card in the first hand, including both its rank and its suit.
  2. Wait while the hand disappears and returns with one card missing.
  3. Compare the remaining cards with the hand you remember.
  4. Pick the missing card from the five choices to continue through the rounds.

A quick example

If you see the ace of hearts, four of clubs, seven of spades and queen of diamonds, and the returning hand contains the ace, seven and queen, the missing card is the four of clubs.

Tips for your first game

  • Remember the suit as well as the number: two cards with the same rank are different cards.
  • Group the hand by suits or ranks in your mind while it is visible.
  • Check what remains before choosing. The surviving cards help you narrow down what disappeared.

The Daily and more puzzles

The Daily hand sequence is shared by everyone playing that puzzle date. More puzzles provides extra runs with difficulty options. Compare Daily results on friends and global leaderboards.
