Daily Sudoku for iPhone & iPad

Sudoku is the classic number-placement puzzle: fill a 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Puzzle Lantern gives everyone the same daily grid, with pencil marks, undo and optional conflict highlighting.

How to play Sudoku

  1. Start with the printed digits. They are fixed clues and cannot be changed.
  2. Choose an empty cell and check its row, column and 3×3 box. A digit already present in any of those places cannot go in that cell.
  3. Use pencil marks to keep track of possible digits. Fill a digit when the clues leave just one possibility.
  4. Continue until all 81 cells are filled without repeating a digit in a row, column or box.

A quick example

If a row contains 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9, its remaining cell must be 5. In a less complete row, check the column and box too: they can eliminate digits that the row alone cannot.

Tips for your first game

  • Begin with rows, columns or boxes that already have many filled cells.
  • Look for a digit that has only one possible position within a box, even when that cell has several pencil marks.
  • After placing a digit, revisit the cells sharing its row, column and box. One placement can unlock several more.

The Daily and more puzzles

The Daily is one shared grid for that puzzle date, so you can compare results on friends and global leaderboards. More puzzles offers additional Sudoku rounds with difficulty options when you want to keep playing.
