Word Sense: Daily Word Puzzle with Clues

Word Sense is a daily word puzzle in Puzzle Lantern. Find three hidden words from sentence prompts, using context to work out each answer. Reveal more clues when you need them and aim to solve with fewer clues.

How to play Word Sense

  1. Read the sentence and work out what kind of word could fit the missing space.
  2. Enter the word you think matches the prompt.
  3. If you need help, reveal another clue. Clues can include a synonym, a definition or information about the letters.
  4. Work through all three words and compare your Daily result with friends.

A quick example

For an illustrative prompt such as “The path was ___ after the rain”, several words might fit at first. A clue such as “hard to grip” would narrow the meaning towards “slippery”. The extra clues help distinguish the intended word from other plausible answers.

Tips for your first game

  • Use the sentence grammar to decide whether you need a noun, verb or adjective.
  • Consider the meaning of the whole sentence before focusing on individual letters.
  • Treat each clue as an additional constraint and check that your guess fits all the clues you have revealed.

The Daily and more puzzles

Each Daily contains three words, and everyone playing that puzzle date gets the same challenge. Word Sense currently offers the Daily puzzle; additional More puzzles rounds are available in Sudoku, Recall and Vanish.

Play on iPhone or iPad

Word Sense is part of the Puzzle Lantern app for iPhone and iPad. Download the app from the App Store, open it and choose Word Sense. There is no signup form or password. These puzzles are played in the app; this page is a guide to the game.
